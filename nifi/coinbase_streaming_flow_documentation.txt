COINBASE REAL-TIME STREAMING PIPELINE

Source:
Coinbase Advanced Trade WebSocket
wss://advanced-trade-ws.coinbase.com

Streaming Platform:
Apache NiFi

Flow:
1. ConnectWebSocket connects to Coinbase.
2. GenerateFlowFile creates BTC-USD ticker subscription.
3. PutWebSocket sends the subscription.
4. JoltTransformJSON transforms incoming ticker events.
5. InvokeHTTP inserts JSONEachRow records into ClickHouse.

Destination:
ClickHouse
Database: bronze
Table: coinbase_ticker

Verified ingestion:
85,335 records

Architecture:
Coinbase WebSocket -> Apache NiFi -> ClickHouse Bronze
